About

About Redlighte

Redlighte is an AI assistant focused on a clean, lightweight experience. It is designed around a simple idea: give people a place to ask questions, explore ideas and create useful content without unnecessary complexity.

How it works

How Redlighte works

You send a message through the Redlighte interface. The request is sent to the Redlighte service, processed by the configured AI system, and the resulting response is returned to the conversation. The exact model or provider may change over time as the service evolves.

Experience

The experience

Redlighte is designed for web and mobile screens, with a focused interface, conversation history and simple controls. The goal is clarity first: fewer distractions, faster interaction and an interface that stays understandable.

Creator

Creator

Redlighte is created and developed by Sepehr. The project is independently designed and developed with the goal of building a simple, useful and modern AI experience.

Usage

Rules of use

Use Redlighte lawfully and responsibly. Do not use the service to harm people, violate privacy, impersonate others, distribute illegal material, abuse the service, attempt unauthorized access, or interfere with its availability or security.

Limitations

AI limitations

AI responses can be incorrect, incomplete, outdated or misleading. Do not treat Redlighte as a substitute for qualified professional advice. Check important information independently, especially for medical, legal, financial, safety or other high-impact decisions.

Privacy

Privacy & account safety

Redlighte may store information necessary to provide its features, subject to its current technical implementation and privacy practices.

Fair use
