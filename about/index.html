About

What is Redlighte?

Redlighte is an AI assistant focused on a clean and lightweight experience. The core idea is simple: provide a place to ask questions, explore ideas and create useful content without unnecessary complexity.

How it works

How Redlighte works

You send a message through the Redlighte interface. The request is sent to the Redlighte service, processed by the configured AI system, and the response is returned to the conversation. The model or provider may change as the service evolves.

Experience

The experience

Redlighte is designed for web and mobile screens, focusing on a clean interface, simple controls and an understandable interaction with AI. The goal is less distraction and clearer, faster interaction.

Rules

Rules of use

Use Redlighte lawfully and responsibly. Do not use it to harm people, violate privacy, impersonate others, distribute illegal material, abuse the service, attempt unauthorized access, or interfere with its availability or security.

Limitations

AI limitations

AI responses can be incorrect, incomplete, outdated or misleading. Redlighte is not a substitute for qualified professional advice. Independently verify important information, especially for medical, legal, financial, safety or other high-impact decisions.

Privacy

Privacy & account safety

Keep your account credentials private and use the service only from trusted devices and environments. Do not submit passwords, payment details, private keys or highly sensitive information without a clear service requirement. Information necessary to provide service features may be stored according to the current technical implementation and privacy practices.

Fair use

Fair use

Do not overload, scrape, reverse engineer or probe the service without authorization, and do not attempt to bypass access controls or disrupt Redlighte or supporting services. Automated use should respect reasonable limits and service rules.

Availability

Availability & changes

Redlighte is an evolving project. Features, models, limits, service status and interface details may change, become temporarily unavailable or be discontinued.

Acceptance

Acceptance of these rules

By using Redlighte, you agree to use the service responsibly and follow these rules. If you do not agree with them, please do not use the service. These rules govern service use and do not replace applicable law.
